The Strategy

Businesses without a website are losing customers every day to competitors who show up on Google. Your cold email doesn't sell the website — it sells the problem they're already feeling. Lead with that pain, offer a free look, then close with the session.

1

Find them. Google Maps searches like "restaurant harbor springs", "hair salon petoskey" etc. — businesses with no website listed, or just a Facebook page link.

2

Verify. Confirm they have no website (or an outdated one). Note their business name and owner name if visible on Google Business or Facebook.

3

Personalize and send. Use the matching template below. Swap in the owner's first name, the business name, and one specific detail you noticed about them.

4

Follow up. Send follow-up #1 at day 3 and follow-up #2 at day 7. Most closes come from the follow-up, not the first email.

Quick Reference — How to Use These

Finding no-website businesses

Search Google Maps for each business type + "Harbor Springs" or "Petoskey." Businesses with no website link on their listing are your targets.

Getting their email

Check their Google Business profile, Facebook page About section, or call and ask for the owner's email. "I have something I'd like to send [Owner] — what's the best email?"

Subject line is everything

The subject lines here are already written for open rates. Don't change them unless you have a specific detail to add. Keep the business name in the subject — it doubles open rates.

Personalizing each email

Replace every bracket: [First Name], [Business Name]. Then add one sentence of real observation — something you noticed on their Google listing or Facebook. That one sentence is what separates you from spam.

What to offer in reply

When they say "sure, show me" — send a one-page mockup or a Loom video walkthrough of what their site could look like. Then close with the $197 session to build it together.

Tracking your outreach

Keep a simple spreadsheet: Business Name | Owner | Email | Date Sent | Follow-up 1 | Follow-up 2 | Status. Work through all 25 before circling back on replies.
