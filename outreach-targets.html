What to Look For on Each Listing

When you open a Google Maps listing, the left panel shows: Phone, Website, Directions, Hours. If there is no "Website" button, or if clicking it goes to a Facebook page — that business is your target.

Also look for listings where the only web presence is a Facebook URL in the "Website" field — Facebook pages are not real websites and do not rank on Google. These are prime opportunities.
